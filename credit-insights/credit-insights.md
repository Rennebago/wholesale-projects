# Goal

Define the MVP requirements for Credit Report Insights in the internal WIB experience used by Wholesale Debt Consultants (DCs). The goal is to help DCs quickly understand a client’s credit history and financial situation during discovery, without relying on the client-facing Financial Analysis (FA).

# User Story

As a Debt Consultant, I need to walk a potential client looking for debt relief through their credit report and financial standing. Today, I'm able to send them a financial analysis breaking down their debt situation and debt relief program details, but I don't have quick insights into their credit report to make the conversation easier during my discovery, unless I open their financial analysis.

With my own credit insights, an ai-driven support agent, I could quickly get talking points to open with as well as key moments in the deeper analysis to speak to as the conversation continues.
