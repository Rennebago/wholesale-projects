### Goal

Define the MVP requirements for Credit Report Insights in the internal WIB experience used by Wholesale Debt Consultants (DCs). The goal is to help DCs quickly understand a client’s credit history and financial situation during discovery, without relying on the client-facing Financial Analysis (FA).

This is a product and workflow discovery ticket. It should produce an agreed MVP scope, lens-level data requirements, workflow triggers, integration boundary, failure behavior, and UI placement recommendation. It does not implement the feature.
